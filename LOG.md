| 2026-10-28 | `da479cca` | Correct the BSRAM inference claim; RV32I is 40 instructions, not 37 | `LEARNING.md`, `SPEC.md` | Two factual errors found while reviewing the route against a pipeline endpoint. **(1) BSRAM.** `LEARNING.md` claimed `assign dout = mem[addr]` is placed in BSRAM, which was the stated reason a single-cycle core needs no hand-written memory. Measured on this board: BSRAM inference requires the read address to come from a **register**. A registered address infers BSRAM at 1024 words (10 LUT4, 272 MHz); an ALU-computed address at the same depth **fails place and route** (`Unable to find legal placement for cell mem.0.1 of type RAM16SDP4`).
